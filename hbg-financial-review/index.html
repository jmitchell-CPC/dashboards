What changed in this update. (1) August is now complete. April and May carry no management fee (about $21K at 5%) and later packages restated Jan–May slightly, which is why the August 31 balance sheet shows year-to-date net income of $108,853 against the $141,778 summed here; the reconciliation is on the "YTD vs. Budget" page.
